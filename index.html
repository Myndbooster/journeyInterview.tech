The story arc

Six lenses. One consequential choice.

The structure is designed to reveal the thinking behind the outcome — not simply retell the outcome itself.

01 / THE FORK

What were the two paths?

We reconstruct the actual decision rather than narrating it with hindsight.

02 / WHAT YOU KNEW

What information did you have then?

What was clear, what was missing, and who influenced your thinking?

03 / THE UPDATE

What did you believe that turned out to be wrong?

We look for the assumption that experience forced you to revise.

04 / THE PRICE

What did choosing this require you to give up?

Time, certainty, status, money, relationships, or another opportunity.

05 / THE ROAD NOT TAKEN

Where might the other path have led?

Success is not the only lens. Tradeoffs make the story more honest.

06 / THE HAND-OFF

What question would you give the next alum?

Not a slogan — a useful question for someone facing a similar crossroads.

FAQ

What to expect.

How much time does it take?

Plan for approximately 25–30 minutes for the Zoom interview. There is minimal preparation required beforehand.

Do I need to prepare answers or slides?

No. We will come prepared with context and guide the conversation. If helpful, you may optionally share a headshot, short bio, or photographs connected to a defining moment.

Is this a company or promotional interview?

No. The focus is the person, the decision, and the thinking behind the journey. Company context may appear where relevant, but the conversation is not designed as a corporate pitch.

How will the interview be used?

The conversation may be developed into an editorial feature for the alumni website, with selected short video clips, quotes, or supporting story elements where appropriate.

What happens next if I’m interested?

Reply to your invitation email and we will coordinate a convenient time for a 25–30 minute Zoom conversation.
